## Article 1 — Organization
The competition is organized by the Association sportive fictive, on the Sunday morning shown on the home page.

## Article 2 — Participants
It is open to children from CP to CM2 at a participating school, within the places available. Beyond that, registrations go to the waiting list.

## Article 3 — Responsibility
The child stays under the full responsibility of the accompanying adult for the whole event.

## Article 4 — Events and ranking
Each event earns points according to the performance. Rankings are by grade and gender, on the total of points. Ties are broken by the number of first places, then of top-3 places, then in favour of the youngest.

## Article 5 — Personal data
The data collected is only used to organize the competition and is erased 30 days after the event.
