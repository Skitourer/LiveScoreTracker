# Hockey

## Equipment

Sticks, ball, cones, small goal, stopwatch.

## How it works

Slalom with the ball, then a shot at goal. The timer stops at the shot.

## Attempts and fouls

**2 attempts.** Goal scored: tick the box, 3 seconds are taken off. Missed cone: foul.
