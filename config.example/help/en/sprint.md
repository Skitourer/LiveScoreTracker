# Sprint

## Equipment

Starting and finishing cones, stopwatch (or the phone's timer).

## How it works

Two children start together, standing behind the line. Start on "Ready… Go!". Stop the timer when the chest crosses the line.

## Attempts and fouls

**1 attempt.** False start: start again without writing anything. Fall or stop: foul.
