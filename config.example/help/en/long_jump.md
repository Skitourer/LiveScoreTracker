# Long jump

## Equipment

Sand pit or mat, rake, measuring tape.

## How it works

Free run-up, take-off behind the board. Measure to the mark closest to the board.

## Attempts and fouls

**2 attempts.** Stepping over the board: foul. At this workshop a foul does not count: the child does the attempt again.
