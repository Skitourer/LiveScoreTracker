# Obstacle course

## Equipment

Hoops, hurdles, mats, cones, stopwatch.

## How it works

Show the course before each group. The timer starts on the signal and stops at the finish line.

## Attempts and fouls

**1 attempt.** Missed obstacle: the child goes back to do it. Giving up: foul.
