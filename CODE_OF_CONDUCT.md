# Code of conduct

LiveScoreTracker is made by and for volunteers who organize a sports day for children. We want everyone who takes part in the project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — to feel welcome and respected.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are volunteers, beginners or not native English speakers.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Accept that others may disagree, and explain your point of view calmly.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Respect privacy: never share personal data about anyone, and in particular about children, in the project's spaces.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and remarks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a friendly, family-oriented volunteer setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub (a private message or, if it concerns security, the [security policy](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
